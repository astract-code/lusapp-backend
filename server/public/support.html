Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Open the app and tap "Sign Up". Enter your name, email address, and a password. You'll receive a verification email — tap the link inside to activate your account.

How do I reset my password?

On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password" and enter your email. We'll send you a link to reset your password.

How do I add a race to my calendar?

Go to the Discover screen, find a race you're interested in, and tap it to open the detail view. Tap "Add to Calendar" to save it to your race schedule.

How do I join a group?

Navigate to the Groups tab and browse available groups. Tap a group to view details, then tap "Join Group." Password-protected groups will ask you for the group password.

How do I delete my account?

Go to your Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This will permanently remove your account and all associated data within 30 days. This action cannot be undone.

I found a bug — how do I report it?

Please email us at support@lusapp.com with a description of the issue, your device model, and iOS/Android version. Screenshots are always helpful!

Can I submit a race to be listed in the app?

Yes! In the Discover screen, tap the "+" button to submit a race. All submissions are reviewed by our team before being published.
